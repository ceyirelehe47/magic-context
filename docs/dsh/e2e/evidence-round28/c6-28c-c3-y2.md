    leases=[{holder dsh-historian:…:4847:a8cca8e6, owner_pid 4847, expires_at +300 s}]

pid 4847 is the NEW y-2 process, not the dead holder: the dead lease was taken
over immediately (no 5-min TTL wait), both y-2 historian passes ran `noop`, and
after the turn there is no live lease and `compartment_in_progress = 0`. Every
other Y2 condition holds; `still_dead` is empty. On rc.5 (41839150646) the same
pre-step trigger fires, but by the step's status dump the noop pass has already
released the lease (`leases: []`, busy false). The y-2 log order is identical on
both versions (trigger → native check → step_start).

Classification: **timing assumption of the Y2 check** (the probe samples while
the new process's own pass still holds its fresh lease); Magic's behaviour
(dead-holder takeover, no wait, nothing left behind) is correct on alpha.3.
Why alpha.3 starts the step sooner is **static inference** (faster pre-step on
alpha.3), not reproduced locally. Not changed: the check is not relaxed (user
rule); whether Y2 should accept a busy probe whose only lease belongs to the
live new process is listed for the user.
