§8§ Cuckoo filters win when you need to delete specific items, since a plain Bloom filter cannot remove an entry without risking false negatives. They also win at low target false-positive rates (roughly under 1–3 percent), where storing short cuckoo fingerprints takes less space per item than the bit array a Bloom filter would need for the same accuracy, and lookups touch a single bucket rather than k scattered bits.
